## 中文

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge，建议使用 **AE2 15.4.11、LDLib 1.0.52.a 和 GuideME 20.1.15**。

### 新增与优化

- 完善超大数量的精确计算，统一合成规划、循环求解、执行记账、存储统计与工作站费用计算，减少大数量运算中的溢出与临时对象分配。
- 优化合成预览响应：首选路线缺料时优先展示完整材料清单，并继续搜索可执行的替代路线。
- 限制找到可执行方案后的额外优化时间，搜索结束后统一展示最终方案，避免确认界面中的方案反复变化。
- 新增递归循环合成图，按从左到右的层级展示种子、外部补料、加工步骤、最终产物与剩余材料，支持查看精确数量和配方详情。
- 统一合成图材料详情卡片，清晰展示请求、库存、待合成与缺料数量，完善大数量提示及多语言显示。
- 合成报告摘要增加样板总数与循环总数；尚未完成计算时保留“已算字节”，悬停可查看精确数值。
- 支持将完整合成材料清单导出为高清 PNG；大型清单自动均衡排列为多栏表格，并拼接为一张图片。
- 优化大型合成报告同步，优先传输材料表，再分页压缩传输合成图，减少重复排序与遍历。
- 计算主机任务按实时加工状态分组排列，优先展示运行与排队任务，并保留受阻任务及其原因。
- 将样板上传入口统一为编码终端第五个页签，改善布局对齐与配方查看器避让。

### 修复

- 修复批量合成供能计算中的浮点误差导致能源不足误判的问题。
- 修复超大订单跨批次累计产物与返还物时可能出现的数量溢出。
- 改善循环合成的启动种子与持续耗材分析，区分“无法获得种子”和“种子可制作但整单原料不足”，补全具体缺料信息。
- 修复大型循环订单仅显示数量不足、遗漏实际材料缺口的问题。
- 修复未完成报告被误显示为零字节或完整总量的问题。
- 完善合成提交失败提示，明确显示本次未能提取的材料余量，并提供重试与重新计算说明。
- 适配 GTL 两类无限存储盘的精确库存，正确读取超出常规数量范围的存量，保留有限库存的消耗语义。
- 修复合成图最终产物与剩余物料的重复分配及连线数量问题。

### 兼容性说明

- 合成报告网络协议已更新，客户端与服务端需要同步升级。

## English

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge. Recommended versions: **AE2 15.4.11, LDLib 1.0.52.a, and GuideME 20.1.15**.

### Added and improved

- Improved exact arithmetic for very large quantities across crafting planning, cycle solving, execution accounting, storage statistics, and workstation costs, reducing overflow risks and temporary allocations.
- Improved crafting preview responsiveness: show the complete material list when the preferred route lacks ingredients, then continue searching for an executable alternative.
- Bounded optional optimization after finding an executable plan and publish the final plan when the search finishes, preventing repeated plan changes on the confirmation screen.
- Added recursive cycle graphs with left-to-right stages showing seeds, external inputs, processing steps, final outputs, and remaining materials, including exact quantities and recipe details.
- Unified material detail cards in crafting graphs to display requested, stored, scheduled, and missing quantities, with improved large-number tooltips and translations.
- Added pattern and cycle totals to report summaries. Incomplete calculations retain a clearly labeled calculated byte count, with exact values available in tooltips.
- Added high-resolution PNG export for complete crafting material lists. Large lists are balanced across multiple table panels and stitched into one image.
- Improved large crafting report synchronization by sending material tables first, followed by compressed graph pages, while reducing repeated sorting and traversal.
- Computation host tasks are grouped by live processing state, prioritizing running and queued tasks while retaining blocked tasks and their reasons.
- Standardized pattern upload as the fifth tab of the Pattern Encoding Terminal, improving alignment and recipe viewer exclusion areas.

### Fixed

- Fixed floating-point rounding causing false insufficient-energy errors during batch crafting.
- Fixed possible overflow when accumulating outputs and returned ingredients across batches of very large orders.
- Improved cycle seed and ongoing ingredient analysis, distinguishing unavailable seeds from craftable seeds with insufficient total materials, and reporting concrete shortages.
- Fixed large cycle orders reporting insufficient quantity without identifying the missing materials.
- Fixed incomplete reports appearing as zero-byte plans or complete byte totals.
- Improved crafting submission failure tooltips to show the amount that could not be extracted, with guidance on retrying and recalculating.
- Added exact inventory support for two GTL infinite storage cell types, reading quantities beyond the normal range while preserving finite-stock consumption semantics.
- Fixed duplicate allocation and incorrect connection quantities for final outputs and remaining materials in crafting graphs.

### Compatibility

- The crafting report network protocol has changed. Update both client and server together.
